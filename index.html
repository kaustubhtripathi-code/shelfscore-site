Every tracker tells you what you ate. Turn on the Plan tab and ShelfScore tells you what to eat next — with your day’s actual movement already subtracted. The number is computed on your phone, not generated. The plan maths is ordinary arithmetic with a hard floor under it — it will not build an unsafe deficit, and it caps how fast it will let you go.
