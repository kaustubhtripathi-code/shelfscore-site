ShelfScore

Privacy Policy

Effective 10 July 2026 · applies to the ShelfScore Android app

The short version

ShelfScore has no accounts and no server of its own storing your data. Your scans, food diary, profiles and photos live on your device. The app talks to two outside services to do its job — a public food database and an AI model — described below. We don't sell data, and we show no ads.

What stays on your device

  • Scan history and food diary — stored locally in the app's database.
  • Profiles (health goals, allergen watch-lists, daily targets, household members) — stored locally.
  • Photos you capture (dishes, labels, receipts) — the most recent are kept in the app's private storage so your history can show them.
  • Usage analytics — a local event log on your device. Nothing is transmitted to us.
  • Health Connect readings (Plan tab only) — steps, calories, heart rate, sleep, exercise sessions and weight are read on the device to work out your daily target. The readings themselves are never transmitted. Only a calorie-and-protein number derived from them is sent when you ask for a suggestion. The one thing written back to Health Connect is a weigh-in you type yourself. Revoke any of it any time in the Health Connect app; the Plan keeps working without it.
  • Your plan and lab results (Plan tab only) — weight, goal, target date and any lab report you add are stored locally, and lab photos sit in the app's private storage.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of the above.

What leaves your device, and to whom

  • Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org): when you scan a barcode or search, the barcode/search text is sent to this public, non-profit food database to fetch product data. See their privacy policy.
  • Google Gemini API: when you use AI photo analysis (dish photos, label rescue, receipt audits), the photo is sent to Google's Gemini API for processing and the analysis is returned. Receipts can contain personal details — crop or skip anything you'd rather not send. See Google's privacy policy. (Some app versions route this through our relay server, which forwards the image to Google and does not store it.)
  • Moonshot Kimi API (Plan tab only): when you photograph or type a meal, add a lab report, or ask what to have for dinner, that text or photo — plus the calorie budget for the meal being planned — is sent to Moonshot's Kimi model for processing and the result is returned. Lab reports contain personal health information; skip anything you would rather not send. See Moonshot's terms. The Plan tab is opt-in: none of this happens unless you open it and set a plan up.
  • Contributing a product (optional, explicit button): if you choose “Add to Open Food Facts”, the product's name, ingredients and nutrition values are published to the public Open Food Facts database under an open license. No personal information about you is included.

Affiliate links

“Buy” links on suggested alternatives may be affiliate links (e.g. Amazon Associates, EarnKaro). If you buy through them we may earn a commission at no cost to you. Rankings and health scores are never influenced by this.

Children

ShelfScore is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Not medical advice

Scores and flags are decision support built from public nutrition data and heuristics — not medical advice. Consult a professional for dietary decisions tied to a medical condition.
